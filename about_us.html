About Ishapore Arms Factory

The Rifle Factory Ishapore, established in 1801, is one of India's oldest arms manufacturing facilities. Located in West Bengal, it was originally set up during British rule to produce firearms for the army.

After independence, the factory continued to serve the Indian Armed Forces, producing well-known rifles such as the Lee-Enfield variants and the Self Loading Rifle (SLR).

Today, Ishapore remains an important part of India’s defense manufacturing ecosystem, contributing to the production of small arms and related equipment.
