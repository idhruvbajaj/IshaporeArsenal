      Protective equipment grouped in the same premium layout, with emphasis on clarity and quick reading.

      Lightweight soft body armour designed to stop handgun rounds. Comfortable for extended wear. Can't stop rifle rounds.

      Ballistic Vest (Level IV)

      Heavier and more restrictive than a Level IIIA vest but capable of stopping armor-piercing bullets and other high-powered rounds.

      Price: ₹95,000 + GST

      A specialized, lightweight, bullet-resistant headgear used primarily by Indian Army and Paramilitary Forces. Provides frontal protection against rifle rounds and rear protection against handgun rounds.
